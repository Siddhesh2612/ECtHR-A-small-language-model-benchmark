# Attribution and source boundaries

The historical experiments adapted ideas and code from
[coastalcph/lex-glue](https://github.com/coastalcph/lex-glue), Hugging Face
Transformers and PEFT. They are retained as a research archive, not represented
as wholly original library implementations.

The new package follows LexGLUE's ten article outputs and derived no-violation
evaluation convention. It does not vendor the original LexGLUE library.

Dataset citation: Ilias Chalkidis et al. (2022), *LexGLUE: A Benchmark Dataset for
Legal Language Understanding in English*, ACL, pages 4310–4330.
[Paper](https://aclanthology.org/2022.acl-long.297/).

Dataset, pretrained weights, software dependencies and third-party figures have
their own terms. No blanket license is assigned to those materials by this
repository. An open-source license for the author's code remains a publication
decision; no license has been invented during preparation.

Reference papers, other students' dissertations and personal records are not
included in the publication tree.
